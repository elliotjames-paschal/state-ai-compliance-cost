PUBLIC CHAPTER NO. 781 
SENATE BILL NO. 837 
By Pody, Gardenhire, Bowling 
Substituted for: House Bill No. 849 
By Reneau, Bulso, Helton-Haynes, Reedy, Pearson, Todd, Powers 
AN ACT to amend Tennessee Code Annotated, Title 1, relative to certain conditions of personhood. 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF TENNESSEE: 
SECTION 1. Tennessee Code Annotated, Section 1-3-105(a), is amended by deleting 
subdivision (20) and substituting: 
(20) "Person": 
(A) Includes a corporation, firm, company, or association; and 
(8) Does not include artificial intelligence, a computer algorithm, a software 
program, computer hardware, or any type of machine; 
SECTION 2. This act takes effect upon becoming a law, the public welfare requiring it.
