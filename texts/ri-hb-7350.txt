CHAPTER 63 3 
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE COMPANION MODELS 4 
6-63-1. Definitions.  5 
As used in this chapter: 6 
(1)(i) "AI companion" means a system using artificial intelligence, generative artificial 7 
intelligence, and/or emotional recognition algorithms to simulate a sustained human or human-like 8 
relationship with a user by:  9 
(A) Retaining information on prior interactions or user sessions and user preferences to 10 
personalize the interaction and facilitate ongoing engagement with the AI companion;  11 
(B) Asking unprompted or unsolicited emotion -based questions that go beyond a direct 12 
response to a user prompt; and  13 
(C) Sustaining an ongoing dialogue concerning matters personal to the user.  14 
(ii)"AI companion" shall not include:  15 
(A) Any system used by a business entity solely for customer service or to strictly provide 16 
users with information about available commercial services or products provided by such entity, 17 
customer service account information, or other information strictly related to its customer service;  18

(B) Any system that is primarily designed and marketed for providing efficiency 1 
improvements or research or technical assistance; or  2 
(C) Any system used by a business entity solely for internal purposes or employee 3 
productivity.  4 
(2) "AI model" means a component of an information system that implements artificial 5 
intelligence technology and uses computational, statistical, or machine -learning techniques to 6 
produce outputs from a given set of inputs. 7 
(3) "Artificial intelligence", "artificial intelligence technology", or "AI" means a machine-8 
based system that can, for a given set of human -defined objectives, make predictions, 9 
recommendations, or decisions influencing real or virtual environments. 10 
(4) "Emotional recognition algorithms" means artificial intelligence that detects and 11 
interprets human emotional signals in text (using natural language processing and sentiment 12 
analysis), audio (using voice emotion AI), video (using facial movement analysis, gait analysis, or 13 
physiological signals), or a combination thereof. 14 
(5) "Generative artificial intelligence" means a class of AI models that are self-supervised 15 
and emulate the structure and characteristics of input data to generate derived synthetic content 16 
including, but not limited to, images, videos, audio, text, and other digital content.  17 
(6) "Operator" means any person, partnership, association, firm, or business entity, or any 18 
member, affiliate, subsidiary or beneficial owner of any partnership, association, firm, or business 19 
entity who operates or provides an AI companion. 20 
(7) "Person" means any natural person. 21 
(8) "User" means any person who uses an AI companion within the state and who is not an 22 
operator or agent or affiliate of the operator of the AI companion. 23 
6-63-2. Prohibitions and requirements.  24 
(a) It shall be unlawful for any operator to operate or provide an AI companion to a user 25 
unless such AI companion contains a protocol for addressing: 26 
(1) Possible suicidal ideation or self-harm expressed by a user to the AI companion;  27 
(2) Possible physical harm to others expressed by a user to the AI companion; and 28 
(3) When any of those expressions are made, a notification shall be provided to the user 29 
that refers them to crisis service providers such as a suicide hotline, crisis text line, or other 30 
appropriate crisis services as soon as any of those expressions are detected. 31 
(b) Beginning July 1, 2027, operators shall file annual reports with the office of the attorney 32 
general which shall include the number of safety protocol activations and related metrics. The office 33 
of the attorney general shall publish aggregated data on its website. 34

6-63-3. Notifications required.  1 
An operator shall provide a clear and conspicuous notification to a user at the beginning of 2 
any AI companion interaction and at least every three (3) hours for continuing AI companion 3 
interactions hereafter, which states either verbally or in writing that the user is not communicating 4 
with a human. 5 
6-63-4. Enforcement.  6 
(a) The attorney general shall be empowered to enforce this chapter, including the power 7 
to investigate, sue, and seek injunctions against noncompliant AI companion operators for 8 
violations of this chapter.  9 
(b) Violations of this chapter may result in civil penalties up to fifteen thousand dollars 10 
($15,000) per day, with fines directed to suicide prevention programs. 11 
6-63-5. Severability.  12 
If any provision of this chapter is declared unconstitutional or invalid, or the application of 13 
any provision of this chapter to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the constitutionality of 14 
the remainder of the chapter and its applicability to other persons and circumstances shall not be 15 
affected thereby. 16 
SECTION 2. This act shall take effect on January 1, 2027. 17

EXPLANATION 
BY THE LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL 
OF 
A N   A C T  
RELATING TO COMMERCIAL LAW--GENERAL REGULATORY PROVISIONS -- 
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE COMPANION MODELS 
***
This act would create additional safety features for artificial intelligence (AI) companion 1 
technology including provisions addressing suicidal ideation, potential physical harm or financial 2 
harm to others expressed by a user. The act also requires notification that the AI companion does 3 
not have human emotions. 4 
This act would take effect on January 1, 2027. 5
