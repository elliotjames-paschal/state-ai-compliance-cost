3 LONG TITLE
4 General Description:
5 This bill addresses offenses related to child sexual abuse material.
6 Highlighted Provisions:
7 This bill:
8 ▸ defines the term of "apparent child sexual abuse material" to address material created or
9  generated by artificial intelligence that depicts realistic minors;
10 ▸ defines the term of "obscene child sexual abuse material";
11 ▸ amends the definition of "child sexual abuse material" to remove artificially generated
12  material;
13 ▸ creates standalone offenses for:
14  ● possession of apparent child sexual abuse material;
15  ● distribution of apparent child sexual abuse material in certain circumstances; and
16  ● producing, distributing, receiving, or possessing with intent to distribute, obscene
17  child sexual abuse material; and
18 ▸ makes technical and conforming changes.
