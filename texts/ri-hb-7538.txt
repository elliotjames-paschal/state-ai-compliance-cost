ACT 5 
23-106-1. Short title and purpose.     6 
(a) This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the "Use of Artificial Intelligence by 7 
Healthcare Providers Notification Act". 8 
(b) The purpose of this chapter i s to ensure patients are properly notified of the use of 9 
artificial intelligence by healthcare providers. 10 
23-106-2. Definitions.     11 
As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the following meanings: 12 
(1) "Artificial Intelligence " or "AI" means any technology that can simulate human 13 
intelligence including, but not limited to, natural language processing, training language models, 14 
reinforcement learning from human feedback and machine learning systems. 15 
(2) "Healthcare facility" shall have the same meaning as § 23-17-2. 16 
(3) "Healthcare provider " means physicians, physician assistants, dentists, registered 17 
nurses, licensed practical nurses, advanced practice registered nurses, nursing assistants, or any 18

23-106-3. Notifications required.     2 
Any and all healthcare providers and healthcare facilities that employ artificial intelligence 3 
("AI") to document in-person or telehealth visits shall notify patients of the use of AI for that sole 4 
purpose and review the AI-generated documentation for accuracy after the visit. 5 
SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon passage. 6
