3 LONG TITLE
4 General Description:
5 This bill requires the State Board of Education to create model policies on the use of
6 technology and artificial intelligence in a public school classroom.
7 Highlighted Provisions:
8 This bill:
9 ▸ defines terms;
10 ▸ requires the State Board of Education (state board) to include certain technology
11  standards in core education standards;
12 ▸ creates certain requirements for a local education agency (LEA) related to the use of
13  technology in the classroom;
14 ▸ requires the state board to create a model policy on:
H.B. 273 Enrolled Copy
15  ● the balanced use of technology in the classroom; and
16  ● the use of artificial intelligence in the classroom;
17 ▸ creates certain requirements for specific grade levels when using technology in the
18  classroom;
19 ▸ exempts certain groups from the grade level requirements when using technology in the
20  classroom;
21 ▸ creates a reporting requirement;
22 ▸ gives authority to the state board to create rules;
23 ▸ requires LEAs to provide additional resources to certain students; and
24 ▸ allows public high schools to create and offer a sandbox artificial intelligence course.
25 Money Appropriated in this Bill:
26 None
27 Other Special Clauses:
28 This bill provides a special effective date.

288 Section 3.  Section 53G-7-229 is enacted to read:
289 53G-7-229  (Effective  07/01/26). Resources for a student with a
290  technology-related learning difficulty.
291 (1)  As used in this section:
292 (a)  "Instructional technology" means the same as that term is defined in Section
293 53G-7-228.
294 (b)(i)   "Technology-related learning difficulty" means a condition where a student
295 experiences a pattern of difficulty learning through the use of instructional
296 technology.
297 (ii)  "Technology-related learning difficulty" does not include a temporary or
298 situational difficulty with technology use that is unrelated to a learning need that
299 is:
300 (A)  diagnosed; or
301 (B)  identifiable.
302 (2)  An LEA shall create a resource plan on how to accommodate a student that
303 demonstrates a technology-related learning difficulty.
304 (3)  An LEA may:
305 (a)  provide additional resources and accommodations to a student that demonstrates a
306 technology-related learning difficulty, including:
307 (i)  access to printed materials;
308 (ii)  alternative assignment formats;
309 (iii)  access to a technology resource center;
310 (iv)  access to an educational professional with expertise in technology and
311 technology-related learning difficulties; and
312 (v)  extra time for technology-based tasks; and
313 (b)  provide a student with a technology-related learning difficulty access to alternative
314 learning pathways, including:
315 (i)  hands-on, project-based learning;
316 (ii)  book-based curriculum; and
317 (iii)  verbal instruction.
