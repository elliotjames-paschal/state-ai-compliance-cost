3 LONG TITLE
4 General Description:
5 This bill modifies provisions related to the Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy and the
6 Artificial Intelligence Learning Laboratory Program.
7 Highlighted Provisions:
8 This bill:
9 ▸ defines terms;
10 ▸ modifies duties of the Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy;
11 ▸ modifies the Artificial Intelligence Learning Laboratory Program;
12 ▸ modifies provisions related to regulatory agreements; and
13 ▸ makes technical and conforming changes.
