3 LONG TITLE
4 General Description:
5 This bill addresses provisions related to offenders.
6 Highlighted Provisions:
7 This bill:
8 ▸ allows a court to order the Division of Adult Probation and Parole to supervise an
9  individual convicted of class B misdemeanors under certain circumstances;
10 ▸ requires the Board of Pardons and Parole (board), if considering whether to parole an
11  offender who has previously been paroled and had the offender's parole revoked due to a
12  new criminal offense, to consider the facts and circumstances of the new criminal
13  offense in determining whether the offender should be paroled again;
14 ▸ allows the board, with certain exceptions, to only consider a pardon for an offender who
15  has committed an offense that requires the offender to register on the Sex, Kidnap, and
16  Child Abuse Offender Registry (the registry) if:
17  ● for a sex offender required to register on the registry for 10 years, 10 years have
18  passed from when the sex offender entered the community after the sex offender's
19  conviction; or
20  ● for a sex offender required to register on the registry for the sex offender's lifetime, 20
21  years have passed from when the sex offender entered the community after the sex
22  offender's conviction;
23 ▸ does not allow an offender who has committed an offense that requires the offender to
24  register on the registry at the time the offender is sentenced to receive a certificate of
25  eligibility of expungement from the Bureau of Criminal Identification; and
26 ▸ adds to the requirements for the board to meet before the board may parole an offender
27  before the offender's minimum term of imprisonment has been met.
