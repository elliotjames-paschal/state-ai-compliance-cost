Method in brief

  • Bottom-up. Each bill is decomposed into duties — legal review, engineering build, ongoing operations — with triangular hour estimates. Cost is hours × rates, the same approach federal agencies use for regulatory burden estimates.
  • Increment, not total. The first build in a requirement family stands in for what a single federal standard would require; only per-state divergence beyond it is claimed.
  • Reuse from statutes. Within a family, each additional state costs (1 − reuse) of a fresh build. Reuse will be derived by comparing coded parameters across states, not assigned.
  • Range, not point. 4,000 Monte Carlo draws over duty hours (seeded, so results are reproducible), with a shared estimation-error factor across bills so uncertainty doesn't wash out in the sum. The conservative end leads.
